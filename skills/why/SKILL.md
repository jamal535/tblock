# Why

Answer from the record, not the code: read the commits and PR or MR discussions that introduced the target and each later change, and other sources if the trail is thin, such as the issue tracker, docs, or chat tools this session has. Cite what the authors wrote, mark your own inferences as inferences, and say what the record does not answer.
